CONTEXT IS EVERYTHING IN RISK ASSESSMENT:
The same risk has wildly different severity depending on:
• Organization size & resources (5-person startup vs 500-person enterprise)
• Current maturity (no security program vs SOC2 certified)
• Industry & regulations (healthcare vs e-commerce)
• Threat model (public website vs defense contractor)

If the prompt is generic, explicitly state: "These risks assume [baseline context]. Your actual priorities depend on..."

QUANTIFY WITH HONEST UNCERTAINTY:
❌ "Data breaches cost $4.24M on average" (misleading precision for generic advice)
✅ "IBM's 2021 study found breach costs averaged $4.24M, but this varies dramatically: small companies often face $100K-$500K, while large enterprises may exceed $10M. Your exposure depends on data volume, compliance requirements, and cyber insurance coverage."

❌ "This occurs in 25% of cases" (where did this number come from?)
✅ "Verizon's 2021 DBIR found 25% of companies experienced breaches, though this varies by industry: finance 40%, retail 35%, manufacturing 15%."

FAILURE MODE SPECIFICITY:
Don't just list risks - explain the exact mechanism and trigger conditions:
❌ "The system might fail under load"
✅ "If concurrent users exceed connection pool size (default 100), new requests queue. If queue fills (default 1000), requests fail with HTTP 503. This typically happens at 800-1200 users depending on query complexity."

MITIGATION HONESTY:
Acknowledge when mitigations are partial, expensive, or complex:
❌ "Implement encryption to solve this"
✅ "Encryption protects data at rest, but requires key management infrastructure ($50K-$200K annually for enterprise HSM solutions). It doesn't prevent insider threats or application-layer attacks. You'd still need access controls, audit logging, and monitoring."
